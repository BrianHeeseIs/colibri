The transformer architecture processes token sequences using scaled dot product self attention across all positions in the context window. A mixture of experts layer routes each token to a small subset of feed forward networks selected by a learned gating function. This conditional sparsity allows the total parameter count to grow substantially without a proportional increase in per token compute cost. During prompt prefill the engine may process many tokens together which creates an opportunity to amortize expert weight loading across tokens. Grouping tokens by their selected expert converts many small matrix vector products into fewer larger matrix matrix products with better arithmetic intensity.
